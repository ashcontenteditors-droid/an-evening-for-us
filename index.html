What I hope we can talk about

Not blame.
Understanding.

I don't want us to sit there trying to prove who was right. I want us to understand where we both went wrong, what we were feeling, and what we didn't know how to say at the time.

01 What really happened between us — from both sides.
02 What hurt, what we misunderstood, and what we should have handled differently.
03 What we've learned since we ended.
04 What we would need from each other to feel safe, heard and respected again.
And if we choose each other again

I don't want us to say
“we'll make it work.”

I want us to know what making it work actually means.

01 How we communicate when we're upset instead of letting things build.
02 How we rebuild trust through actions, not promises.
03 How we handle distance, busy schedules and the seasons when life gets difficult.
04 How we support each other's careers, dreams and individual lives without losing each other in the process.
05 How we keep choosing each other even when being together isn't convenient.

And if our lives ever put kilometres between us again, I want us to have a real plan — not just hope that love will somehow figure everything out for us.
